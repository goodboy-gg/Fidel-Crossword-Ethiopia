Using the App

  • Begin by learning and placing all 231 Fidel letters.
  • Listen to pronunciation audio and practise each Fidel family.
  • Complete easy, mixed and final crossword challenges.
  • Use Settings to adjust sound, text size and preferences.

Privacy

Fidel Crossword Ethiopia does not require an account or sign-in. The app does not collect, store or sell personal information. Learning activities and preferences remain on the user's device.

About

Fidel Crossword Ethiopia is an educational app designed to help learners discover, practise and master the Ethiopian Fidel alphabet at their own pace.
