# Privacy Policy — Fidel Crossword Ethiopia

**Effective date: 25 August 2026**

Fidel Crossword Ethiopia is an educational application for learning and practising the Ethiopian Fidel alphabet.

## Information We Collect

Fidel Crossword Ethiopia does not require users to create an account or sign in, and the app does not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share personal information.

The app may store basic preferences locally on the user’s device, such as app settings. This information remains on the device and is not sent to the developer or third parties.

## Audio and Learning Content

Pronunciation audio and learning activities are provided as part of the app. The app does not record the user’s microphone, collect voice recordings, or upload learning activity to a server.

## Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

Fidel Crossword Ethiopia does not use third-party advertising, cross-app tracking, behavioural advertising, or analytics services that collect personal information.

## Children’s Privacy

The app is designed as an educational learning experience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

## Data Sharing

Because the app does not collect personal information, there is no personal information to sell or share with third parties.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

If the app’s privacy practices change in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.
